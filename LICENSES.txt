PEAK x ROUNDS: Campfire Cards - bundled third-party software and credits

This mod reads the real ROUNDS cards, icons, colours and sounds from YOUR OWN installed copy of ROUNDS
(Landfall Games) while PEAK (Aggro Crab / Landcrab) runs. No files from either game are included in this package.
